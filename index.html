Sheet (gear) = your character sheet with gear only (editable). Total = race base + gear + raid buffs + consumables + weapon oil + passive racials (read-only; the per-build Spellstone / Firestone is not included). Talents differ per build and are shown in each build's details. Timed effects (potions, Power Infusion, Blood Fury, Berserking, Eureka) are applied in the fight when used. Defaults: 500 SP, 5% hit, 10% crit, Int 148, Spirit 54, Stamina 160, MP5 0 (see A58).

One per group; ticking an item unticks the others in its group. The weapon stone and a weapon oil stack. Mana potions and runes are used when at least their amount is missing; the Spellblasting potion follows "Pop cooldowns" (Fight & pets).
