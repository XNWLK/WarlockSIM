Stats

Sheet (gear) = your character sheet with gear only (editable). Total = race base + gear + raid buffs + consumables + weapon oil + passive racials (read-only). Talents differ per build and are shown in each build's details. Timed effects (potions, Power Infusion, Blood Fury, Berserking, Eureka) are applied in the fight when used. Defaults: 500 SP, 5% hit, 10% crit, Int 148, Spirit 54, Stamina 160, MP5 0 (see A58).
